What we fix most in Toronto

Toronto’s older homes mean narrow laneway garages, single doors on original openings and hardware that is often 20+ years old.

  • Broken extension springs

    Common on older single doors. We convert to safer torsion systems where headroom allows.

  • Rusted bottom panels and seals

    Salt and slush eat the bottom section first. Replaced without a whole new door.

  • Openers without safety sensors

    Pre-1993 openers replaced with quiet, modern belt-drive units.

  • Low-headroom laneway garages

    Low-headroom tracks and compact openers for garages built long before minivans.
